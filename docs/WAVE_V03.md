## The owner's Aburame material (summary, from the Spanish wiki and an article)
- One of Konoha's four noble clans and a founding clan. They use chakra-eating insects that live **inside
  the body, under the skin, from birth**, coming and going through the pores.
  - One article says they are **born normal and then offered as a nest** in a rite of implantation.
  - So it is a **hiden** (clan secret knowledge), **not a kekkei genkai**.
- **The pact:** the insects get a home and chakra; the host gets total obedience and can do ninjutsu
  **without hand seals**. Extra insects are carried in jars or gourds.
- **Many species, not just kikaichū:**
  - poisonous nano-insects (Torune's rinkaichū; he infected Tobi's arm and forced him to cut it off);
  - insects that swell and eat the host's flesh if the chakra dose is wrong;
  - underground trackers;
  - more in the anime and a novel.
- **Tracking and scouting:**
  - a female insect is left on a target and the males follow her scent;
  - scout insects come back with information;
  - experts at espionage, because the insects are silent.
- **In combat:** they surround, cut off retreat and drain chakra until the target can't move. A big swarm is
  fast, a small one goes unnoticed. They can eat a jutsu in progress, and draw poison out of an ally.
- **Look and manner:**
  - they hide their eyes with dark glasses and cover most of the body (long coats, high collars, hoods);
  - calm, rational, few emotions; they love to observe and collect insects;
  - they favour wide-area attacks over precise blows.
- **Members:**
  - Shibi (clan head) and Shino (later an Academy teacher);
  - Torune (Root, poison insects) and his father Shikuro;
  - Muta (died in the war), Tatsuma (Root).
- **Rivals of the bee-using Kamizuru** (anime), whom they defeated.
- Reference images the owner shared are in the session scratchpad `images/` (33–42: a kikaichū beetle, a
  swarm, Torune, Aburame coats; Akatsuki and clan crests as mood only).
