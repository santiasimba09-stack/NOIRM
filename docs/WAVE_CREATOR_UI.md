# Next wave: a much richer character creator and a ninja-style UI (owner's brief, 2026-11-19)

**When:** after v0.19.0 is delivered. The owner listed it together with the solid, full-scale jutsu
wave (`docs/WAVE_JUTSU_VISUALS.md`). Both wait for v0.19.0. They can run in parallel, since they touch
different code.

## What the owner said (summary)
- **"Get crazy" with hair and eyes:** make a lot of them, be creative, and make sure they look great
  and fit the character creator.
- **Body types too:** many more choices so the player can personalise their character as fully as
  possible.
- **Redo the UI.** It looks good, but not like something you'd put in a ninja mod. Give the creator
  and **every other screen** (HUD, menus, panels, the Handbook…) a fitting ninja vibe.
- Be creative, but follow our basics:
  - our own art, generated by our tools;
  - no copied franchise or NYDO art;
  - Minecraft-style pixels;
  - readable;
  - the three languages.

## Notes for whoever builds it
- **Starting points:**
  - hair: `tools/skingen`, `docs/HAIR.md` / the hair system;
  - eyes: `tools/skingen/anatomy.py`, `gen_eye_decals.py`, and the eye-atlas test in HairGameTests.
    Keep the owner's eye rules from `docs/SURVIVAL.md`: sharp leaf shape, no lid crease, nothing
    black past the eye;
  - body: the creator's Body/Genetics tabs;
  - UI: `client/ui` and `docs/UI.md` (the UI kit, so a new look can be applied everywhere at once).
- **Ninja vibe ideas to explore:**
  - scroll and parchment panels, ink-brush strokes and borders;
  - wood and paper (shoji) frames, hanko-stamp accents;
  - a brush-style title font in pixel form;
  - forehead-protector metal plates for headers.
- Show options side by side in screenshots before committing to one look.
- Old saves must keep their look; new options are added, never renumbered.
