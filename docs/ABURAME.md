# The Aburame clan: the body is a hive

_Research and design for v0.3 (owner, 2026-11-25; see `WAVE_V03.md`). The Aburame are the first clan to get the
full treatment. **Nothing here is built yet.** Part 1 is the research with sources. Part 2 is the game design
with numbers. Part 3 is the builder's checklist. Everything is in our own words: no wiki text, art or NYDO
content is copied._

**Short version for the owner.** An Aburame's real health bar is their **colony**: a number of living insects
(it starts at about 3,000 and can reach hundreds of thousands at the end) that lives in your body, in jars on
your belt, in a gourd on your back and in hives at home. Your insect techniques **cost insects, not hand seals**:
they fire instantly and **swarms are real things in the world**. They fly out, cling to enemies and eat their
chakra, and they come back to you, fewer than went out. Fire and explosions kill them by the thousand. Feed the
colony chakra and it grows. Breed it and it adapts. Lose it and you are a weak taijutsu fighter in a long coat.

### 1.1 What the clan is

- **A clan of Konohagakure.** `[canon]` (Fanbook pp. 28–29).
  - The Spanish wiki, citing the same fanbook, calls them **one of the "four noble clans"** of Konoha.
    Fan sites list those four as Aburame, Akimichi, Hyūga and Uchiha. `[unverified]` We found no English
    primary source for the phrase. "Founding clan" (from the owner's article) is also `[unverified]`.
- **Hiden, not kekkei genkai.** Their techniques are classed as *Hiden: Aburame Clan* (clan secret
  techniques) on every technique page, never as a kekkei genkai. `[canon]` (DB1 p. 178 for the parent
  technique.) The power is a **pact with insects**, not a bloodline. Two members of the same clan can even
  host different species (see 1.3).
- **The implantation.** Shortly after birth, a child is "offered as a nest" to the insects, which then live
  just under the skin for life and come and go through the pores. `[canon]` (Fanbook pp. 28–29; ch. 70 p6.)
  The owner's article says they are born normal and then implanted in a rite. Both readings agree: the
  insects come after birth, so it is a rite, not inheritance.
- **The pact.** The host gives the insects shelter and chakra to eat. The insects obey every command:
  attack, defence, scouting. The host can do "ninjutsu-like" techniques **without hand seals or chakra
  conversion**. `[canon]` (ch. 70 p6; the Parasitic Destruction Insect Technique page.) The Aburame call
  the lifelong pact itself the **Kikaichū Technique** (寄壊蟲の術, *Kikaichū no Jutsu*). `[canon]` (DB1
  p. 178.) A trading card game calls it B-rank. `[game]` No databook gives any Aburame technique a rank.
- **The name.** 油女 literally means **"oil woman"**. It may also echo *aburamushi* 油虫, **"cockroach"**.
  `[name meaning]`
  - Several members carry storm names: Torune (tornado), Shikuro (cyclone), Tatsuma (*tatsumaki*, tornado),
    Harika (hurricane). `[name meaning]`, `[unverified]` (wiki trivia). A swarm as a storm is a strong theme
    for us.
  - Shibi's favourite saying: *issun no mushi ni mo gobu no tamashii*, **"even a tiny insect has half a
    soul"** (that is, even the small deserve respect). `[canon]` (DB2.)
- **The symbol.** A round body in the centre, a small lobe above and below, three straight lines fanning out
  to each side like legs or wing veins, and an arc closing each side, like a beetle seen from above.
  `[canon]` (Narutopedia's `Aburame Symbol.svg`.) We will draw our own pixel version of the same idea.

### 1.2 How the colony works

### 1.6 Sensing, scouting, tracking, spying, healing

- **Scouting.** Insects spread across a wide area, find the target and report its position. A single kikaichū
  looks like any bug, so it can watch someone and come back to tell. `[canon]` ch. 41 p14
- **Tracking.** The chakra lock-on (ch. 395 p9) and the quieter **female pheromone** (ch. 124 p19).
  Underground, the **shōkaichū** (ch. 515). The **bikōchū** imprints a scent for life `[anime-only]`.
- **Hearing.** Shino can count people in an area with his ear to the ground. `[canon]` ch. 58 p9
- **Spying.** The insects make no noise and no visible motion, so the Aburame are **experts in espionage**.
  Root recruited them (Torune, Tatsuma, Yōji). `[canon]` (wiki overview; Tatsuma DB4: his infiltration skill
  "set the standard" for Root.)
- **Hiding.** *Insect Jamming* fills a sensor's view with false signals `[canon]`. In the anime, Shino was the one
  person White Zetsu couldn't copy, because he never touched them.
- **Messages.** Muta's insects carried a scroll back to headquarters. `[canon]` ch. 517
- **Medicine.** Insects **draw poison out** `[canon]` ch. 137, and **pass chakra to allies** `[anime-only]`
  S-ep. 304.
- **Genjutsu.** The insects' nervous systems are too simple for genjutsu, so they can help against it
  `[anime-only]` ep. 207. But Itachi caught Yōji's insects in Tsukuyomi `[novel]`: a Mangekyō can still do it.
- **Everyday use** `[anime-only]`: Shino's insects clean the Academy at night (students thought it was a
  ghost); Harika uses hers for haunted-house effects and to follow a crumb trail.

### 1.8 Look and manner

- **Eyes always hidden**: round dark glasses (Shino, Shibi), goggles (Harika), a mask with built-in lenses
  (Torune). Even Shino's teammates don't know what his eyes look like. `[canon]`, `[anime-only]`
- **Covered bodies**: long coats with **high, upturned collars** that hide the lower face; hoods (Shino in
  Part II, Shibi in the war); big sleeves; hands in pockets. `[canon]`
- **Gourds and packs**: Shibi's and Muta's big gourds on a harness; Shino's satchel. `[canon]`
- **The owner's reference images**:

  - 36: Torune in Root clothes: a dark high-collar suit, a lens mask, a red sash, black gloves;

## Part 2: our design

### 2.0 The pillars

1. **The colony is a second body.** It's a number you watch, feed, spend, lose and rebuild. It never sits idle:
   it eats your chakra and it grows.
2. **No seals, ever.** Insect techniques fire on the tick you press. They are never slowed, paused or broken by
   hits the way the seal phase is. That's the Aburame's whole edge.
3. **Swarms are real.** Every technique sends a swarm **entity** that has a count, takes damage by losing
   insects, and comes home with the survivors. What you spend is what dies.
4. **Win by area and attrition, not by precision.** Box the target in, cut off escape, drain until they can't
   move. Aburame lose short brawls and win long fights.
5. **Information is a weapon.** Scouting, marks, sensing, jamming and messages are first-class mechanics, not
   flavour.
6. **Fire is terror.** Fire users, explosions and wind are the natural enemies, and the colony **adapts** to
   what kills it.

### 2.1 Who gets a colony

| Who | How |
|---|---|
| **Aburame players** | The implantation happens off-screen at birth. You start with a colony; the creator shows its roll (2.9). |
| **Aburame NPC ninjas** | A colony that fits their class (2.11). |
| **Aburame crew mates** | Keep their colony; they scout, jam and carry messages for you (2.12). |
| **Anyone else** | **No.** It's a clan pact made in infancy. A non-Aburame can only use a **Bikōchū** (2.6) or carry a jar of Aburame scouts that an Aburame crew mate lends them (8 scouts, no combat). |
| **Commands** | `/noirm colony …` (cheats; 3.7). |

### 2.2 The colony as a resource

**One number, four places.** The colony is a count of living insects, kept per species, split between:

| Place | Holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Body** | **Body capacity = 4,000 + 600 × Reserves + 200 × Control** | Reserves 10, Control 10 → 12,000. Reserves 40, Control 30 → 34,000. Reserves 80, Control 60 → 64,000. That's the canon "tens of thousands". |
| **Belt jars** (2 slots max) | 5,000 each | Quick to release from: a jar swarm comes out at +50% speed. |
| **Gourd** (one, worn on the back) | 25,000 | The big reserve, like Shibi and Muta. **If an explosion hits you, the gourd can go off** (2.7). |
| **Hives at home** | 60,000 each, up to 3 hives | 180,000 stored. Feeds back to you when you sleep near it, or by courier (2.5, F). |
| **Out in the field** | — | Swarms you've sent, still alive. They count as part of the colony until they die. |

So a starting player has about **3,000**, a mid-game Aburame carries **40,000–60,000**, and an endgame one has
**200,000+** counting hives. "Hundreds of thousands" becomes our late-game truth.

**Feeding: the pact's price.** The colony in your **body** eats chakra every second:

- **Upkeep:** 0.08 chakra/s per 10,000 insects in the body. At 30,000 that's 0.24/s, a quarter of a beginner's
  1/s regen. Jars and the gourd cost nothing (the insects go dormant), but they don't breed either.
- **Growth:** while the body is below capacity and you're above 20% chakra, the colony breeds at
  **+1.2% of the body colony per minute + 15 flat**. That's about an hour from 3,000 to 6,000, and about 2.5
  hours to 12,000. Breeding costs an extra 0.1 chakra per new insect, paid out of regen.
- **Feeding on enemies:** every **1 chakra your insects drain** from someone else becomes **+6 insects** on top
  of breeding (up to capacity). **Your colony grows by eating your enemies.** This is the main way to grow fast.
- **Starving:** at 0 chakra the colony can't eat. It loses **1% per 10 s** and turns **restless**: techniques
  cost 50% more insects, and swarms come back slower. After 60 s of starving, the insects start **leaving your
  body** (a little black cloud drifts off and dies) until you have chakra again. `[our interpretation]` (canon is
  silent; the hot-bath scene shows they leave a host they don't like.)

**Lifespan and generations.** Kikaichū live a few hours, so we don't track individuals. We track
**generations**: one passes every **10 minutes of play** while the colony is fed. Generations drive adaptation
(below) and let a damaged colony "heal" its make-up fast.

**Adaptation: the colony learns what kills it.** `[our interpretation]` of S-ep. 95/104 and Shino's antibodies.

- Every insect killed by a damage type adds **pressure** to that type: fire, explosion, cold, poison, wind,
  lightning.
- Each generation spends 10 pressure of the leading type for **+2% resistance to it**, up to **40%**.
- In the **Breeding Jar** (2.6) you can also **choose** the type, which doubles the rate.
- The panel shows the "strain" growing: *Heat-hardened 14%*.
- So an Aburame who keeps fighting Uchiha slowly breeds fire-hardened bugs, and the fire user has to adapt too.

**Colony health.** Besides the count, the colony has **Vigour (0–100)**:

| Vigour goes down when you… | Vigour goes up when you… |
|---|---|
| starve | stay fed |
| drink (sake: −30) | eat **wild-grass salad** (Shino's favourite, a new food: +10) |
| bathe in a hot spring (−40, and 60% of the body colony leaves for 60 s, then returns) | rest in the Insect Cocoon |
| sit near insect eaters (frogs, chickens, the pitcher plant item) | keep flowers and honey near a hive |
| carry strong smells (an onion-like "strong food" tag) | |

Vigour scales breeding (×0.25 at 0, ×1.5 at 100) and drain speed (×0.7 to ×1.15). It's the "keep your
insects happy" layer, and it's also how canon's odd weaknesses show up without being a pain.

### 2.4 Casting with no seals

- Every Aburame insect technique has **no `jutsu_seals` file** and a new flag, **`"hiden": "aburame"`**. The
  caster checks the flag: no seal phase, no weaving slowdown, and seals can never break.
- Bound, stunned or held? You can still **send insects** (they act on their own). A shadow-bind victim who is an
  Aburame can still cast Send, Wall, Dome and Clone. This is the clan's signature.
- **Costs are mostly insects.** Each technique "commits" insects. They leave your body as a swarm, and only
  the ones that die are lost. The chakra cost stays small (4–15), because the insects carry their own
  appetite. Upkeep (2.2) is where the chakra really goes.
- **A Sharingan can't copy them** (the user has no insects). The copy shows "needs a colony".
- **Selecting a target:** your lock-on target. With no lock-on, the block or entity you're looking at, up to the
  technique's range.

### 2.5 Swarms and the techniques

#### The swarm entity

**`InsectSwarm`**: one entity per swarm, owned by you.

- **Count:** the insects in it.
- **Species:** which species it carries.
- **Mode:** `travel`, `cloud`, `cling`, `sphere`, `wall`, `dome`, `clone`, `scout`, `jam`, `tether`, `tornado`.
- **Shape and size:** the radius grows with the count (r = 0.6 + count^0.33 / 14 blocks: 2,000 → 1.5,
  10,000 → 2.2, 50,000 → 3.2).
- **Damage = lost insects.** The swarm has no health; every damage point kills insects:

  | Hit by | Insects killed per damage point |
  |---|---|
  | Melee, a weapon or an arrow | 30 (half of one slash goes through the cloud) |
  | Most jutsu | 60 |
  | **Fire** (block fire, fire jutsu, burning entities, lava) | **180** |
  | **Explosions** | **220** |
  | Lightning | 90 |
  | Cold or ice | 90 |

  A sword hit (6) kills about 180 insects. A fireball (10) kills 1,800, so a 2,000 swarm is gone. Adaptation
  (2.2) reduces these numbers.
- **Wind** (wind jutsu, knockback waves) **scatters** a swarm instead: it drops to `cloud` mode for 2 s and loses
  hold of whatever it was clinging to. **Water**: a swarm won't enter water, and diving shakes off clinging insects.
- **Recall** (tap the Swarm key, below): every swarm flies home at 14 blocks/s. The survivors rejoin the body
  (or the jar they came from).
- **Range leash:** a swarm farther than **64 blocks** (scouts: 256) slowly loses its link and returns.
- **Drawing it** (client): **voxel beetles** (3×2×4 px, from ref 33: a charcoal shell `#2b2b30`, a grey shield
  `#6e6a78`, teal eyes `#3fa8a8`). Up to 300 visible per swarm, with level of detail by distance; the rest is a
  dark dust of particles whose density follows the count. Big swarms stream in **branching tendrils** (ref 34).
  Rinkaichū swarms are purple-black (ref 35). A soft "rasping hum" sound loops, louder for bigger swarms.

**The Swarm key** (suggested **M**, for *mushi*, "insect"; B is the beast cloak, and M has no vanilla binding in 1.20.1):

- **tap:** recall all;
- **hold + look at a block:** park your swarms there (an ambush cloud);
- **hold + look at a creature:** send every free swarm at it;
- **double-tap:** toggle the **colony panel** (2.8).

This is the one direct-control key. The techniques themselves go in the normal 4-slot loadout.

#### Draining (the heart of the clan)

Insects that **cling** to a target eat its chakra:

- **Drain rate = 0.5 chakra/s per 1,000 clinging insects** × Vigour × (Sphere ×3).
- **The appetite cap:** a swarm can eat at most **its count ÷ 50 chakra in total** before it's full. A full swarm
  stops draining until recalled; recalling "digests" it, and the colony gains insects from what was eaten.
  So a 2,000 swarm eats at most 40 chakra. Shibi vs the Nine-Tails: a **beast cloak, Sage Mode or any target
  with more than 10× your max chakra overfeeds the insects**, and **they burst** (the swarm loses 5% per second
  instead of draining).
- **Half of what's drained goes to you** (as the existing `chakra_leech` perk already does); the rest becomes
  insects (+6 per chakra).
- **Drained:** a target brought to **0 chakra** is **Drained**: slowness III, no sprinting, no jutsu, no
  dodging for **4 s**, then **Exhausted** (slowness I, −30% damage) until it regains 20% chakra. This is the
  "can't move" of ch. 395.
- **Vanilla mobs** (no chakra) lose **0.3 health/s per 1,000** insects and get Weakness I instead.
- **Being noticed:** a swarm under **800** insects clinging to an NPC is **not noticed**: the NPC doesn't react,
  and a player gets only a faint itch sound. Above that, NPCs try to **shake it off**:
  - a roll or dodge: −25% of the clinging insects;
  - a fire jutsu on themselves: all of them;
  - jumping into water: all of them.

#### Clinging, eating and countering at a glance

- **Big and fast or small and sneaky** (ch. 126): Parasitic Swarm at 1,500 isn't noticed, drains slowly

### 2.6 Hives, breeding and collecting

**Insect Hive** (block; crafted from 4 logs + 4 honeycomb + a chakra paper; looks like a dark clay jar-house
with holes, our own art):

- stores **60,000** insects; **up to 3 per player**;
- breeds stored insects at **+1% per minute**, **×1.5** if 6+ flowers are within 5 blocks and **×1.25** with a
  honey bottle in its slot;
- costs no chakra: **you feed it** by holding use on it (10 chakra → +300 insects), or it slowly eats nearby flowers'
  "nectar" (purely flavour, nothing breaks);
- **sleep within 16 blocks** and your body refills from it in the morning;
- **an explosion next to it kills 50%** and a fire nearby 2% per second, so build it in stone.

**Breeding Jar** (block; glass + clay + a hive frame):

- choose an **adaptation** to breed (×2 pressure rate) or **cross a species** (kikaichū × rinkaichū → Antivenom);
- **raise a Kidaichū Egg** (needs 10,000 kikaichū and a Control 30 host, 1 in-game day);
- **raise a Queen** for the Giant Insect (2.10).

**Bug collecting** (Shino's hobby, DB): **wild insects** are tiny ambient creatures in the world:

- they are particles plus a small model, harmless, despawning;
- you catch them with *Insect Gathering* or a **Bug Net** item (anyone can catch them; only an Aburame breeds
  them in);
- they go in the **Insect Case** item, a collection book of 12 slots that shows where to find each one.

Bred into your colony (once each, at the Breeding Jar), each gives a small **strain**:

### 2.10 Progression

**Hive rank** works like hound levels: named milestones on one curve. The **requirement** is your **total
colony** (body + jars + gourd + hives) **plus a deed**, so it can't be skipped by sitting at home.

| Rank | Colony | Deed | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nestling** | start (≈3,000) | — | Parasitic Swarm, Female Insect, Insect Radar, the Swarm key, 1 species slot, 2 jar slots |
| **Genin hive** | 10,000 | Drain 300 chakra in total from opponents | Insect Clone, Insect Wall, Scout Swarm, Poison Draw, Insect Gathering, Insect Cocoon; the first Insect Hive recipe |
| **Chūnin hive** | 25,000 | Chūnin rank, and a won fight using a female-insect mark | Insect Sphere, Insect Jamming, Insect Jar, Tether, Devour; **species slot 2** (Shōkaichū or Kidaichū); the gourd |
| **Jōnin hive** | 50,000 | Jōnin rank, and 3 adaptations at 10%+ | Insect Tornado, Bug Bite (with kidaichū), Chakra Gift, the Breeding Jar; rinkaichū Poison Cloud |
| **Clan Master** | 120,000 | Beat the Aburame master's trial (below) | **Species slot 3**, 5 clones, the Giant Insect summon (needs a Queen), Jar of Poison (rinkaichū) |
| **Living Hive** (endgame) | 200,000 | A full Insect Case, and a jinchūriki or S-class legend drained to 0 chakra | The endgame forms |

**The master's trial** `[our interpretation]`: the grove's clan head fights you, **only with insects and only
drain**. You win by Draining him first while keeping 50% of your field swarms alive. The owner chooses whether he
is Shibi or an original character (questions).

**Endgame forms** (all three at Living Hive; one toggle and two big techniques):

1. **The Living Hive** (mode, `Kikaichū: Mushi no Karada`, "the insect body") `[our interpretation]`:
   - your skin crawls, dark specks drifting off you;
   - body capacity ×2 and every melee hit you land drops 200 insects onto the target to cling;
   - **Swarm Body**: a hit that would kill you instead **dissolves you into the swarm**. You re-form 12 blocks
     away after 1.5 s, invulnerable in between. It costs **40% of the body colony**, with a 5-minute cooldown;
   - the cost while on: upkeep ×2, and fire damage to **you** +50%.
2. **The Black Sea** (`Kikaichū: Kokkai`, S, ultimate) `[our interpretation]`:
   - **every insect you own, hives included** (by courier), floods a **28-block radius** for 20 s;
   - everything inside is drained ×2 and eaten if it's a jutsu;
   - anyone at 0 chakra inside is held still;
   - you see everything in it through walls; the sky goes dark; a hum you feel in the controller;
   - it costs **60% of the whole colony** (the dead ones), and one in-game day to regrow.
3. **The Insect Legion** (S) `[our interpretation]`:
   - 5 Insect Clones that **fight** (anime clones: 60% of your melee damage, and they cast Parasitic Swarm),
     for 45 s;
   - each burst clone becomes a cling swarm.

Plus the **Giant Insect** mount (Clan Master): it flies (slower than an elytra, steadier), carries 3, dives into
and out of water (the *Boruto* scene), and its bite drains. It's summoned for 30 chakra from a Queen you raised.

**How they fight** (a new `AburameFightGoal`, used by `NinjaFightGoal` when the NPC has a colony):

1. **Keep 12–20 blocks away**, behind cover; never start in melee.
2. Open with a **small swarm** (under 800) plus a **female mark**: the slow, quiet drain.
3. Mark a **ring** of parked swarms around the target (cut off retreat), then **Sphere**.
4. Hit with a fire or explosion? **Clone + retreat + Jamming**, then come back later. **A marked player is
   tracked**: an Aburame who survives the fight can return within 1–3 in-game days with 2 friends (a Bingo-Book-
   style hunter event).
5. Against fire users they hide behind **walls** and prefer to send swarms from angles.

**Scouting:**
- Aburame village guards leave **female marks** on intruders who misbehave;
- patrols send scouts that **reveal sneaking players** within 48 blocks (the Moth strain and Jamming beat them);
- an Aburame in a **team with a Hyūga and an Inuzuka** (Team 8 tracking squads) gets +50% scouting range.

**The Kamizuru rivalry:** when an Aburame and a Kamizuru NPC meet, they fight on sight. A Kamizuru squad is the
enemy most likely to be sent after an Aburame player (honey, bees). That's an "anti-Aburame" foe for free, and a
taste of the Kamizuru's own deep treatment later.

### 2.13 Balance notes

- **Early game:** 3,000 insects is two Parasitic Swarms. A fresh Aburame is weaker up close than other clans
  and stronger at denying an area. Draining is slow until the Sphere at chūnin.
- **Mid game:** the Sphere at 9 chakra/s drains a 150-chakra chūnin in about 17 s if it holds. It is very good
  against people without wind, fire, teleports or big pools. It's countered by every Uchiha (fire) and Fūma
  (wind).
- **Late game:** the Black Sea is an area win button with a day-long cost. The Living Hive's Swarm Body is a
  second life on a 5-minute cooldown.
- **Simulator:** add the colony to `tools/balance/simulate.py` so an Aburame still reaches every rank (the rule
  for v0.19.0).
