# Styles, clans, traits, genjutsu and the Flying Raijin (owner's brief, 2026-11-18)

The owner's words: "work on more styles, much more styles of fighting, play with the stances, movement,
guards… make clans much more significant, research canon clans and make their perks noticeable (for
example, the Uzumaki have an affinity with sealing jutsu, an absurdly big chakra pool, red hair, regen and
longevity)… look through ALL you can to make clans feel more important and have real impact on the
playthrough… traits should not be per clan but more like per player: talents can differ even within
the same clan… make the genjutsu moves feel like a genjutsu: make a scene in the affected player or mob's
mind, like a real genjutsu… make Body Flicker much better and more accurate, and make a Flying Raijin
jutsu. I LOVE THIS JUTSU, so take special attention: make it incredible. A lot of games and mods don't get
the sense of it, and I want us to be the exception. Investigate all you can."

## 1. Many more fighting styles
- Far more styles, each with its own stance, footwork and movement, guard, sub-stances and move set,
  built on the existing engine and the snap key-pose system (`COMBAT_DESIGN.md`).
- Styles should feel different to play, not only to look at: range, speed, guard type (parry-heavy,
  evasive, armoured), how they move.

## 2. Clans that matter
- Research the canon clans and give each one noticeable perks that change a playthrough, for example the
  Uzumaki: an affinity for sealing jutsu, an enormous chakra pool, red hair, fast regeneration and long
  life. Do the same for every canon clan, and give generated clans meaningful perks too.

## 3. Personal traits
- Talents are per player, not per clan: two members of the same clan can have different talents. Clans
  bias the odds; the player's own traits are rolled at creation.

## 4. Genjutsu that feels like genjutsu
- Being caught puts the victim's mind in a scene: the world changes around them (sky, ground, figures,
  sounds), not only a screen tint. Mobs and NPCs caught in one visibly act as if they see something else.
  Counterplay stays (Kai, a strong mind, the Sharingan, a friend's touch).

## 5. Body Flicker, done right
- Faster, more accurate and readable: the real speed-blur dash, with the right timing and feel.

## 6. Flying Raijin (special attention)
- Research it deeply (how it works in canon: the marked kunai and seals, instant space-time travel to any
  mark, the feel and timing of it) and build the best version any game or mod has made.

## Follow-up (owner, 2026-11-18): much bigger clan gaps, and a growing Inuzuka hound
- **Much bigger gaps between clans**, drawbacks included. Example: in Part 1, Kakashi says Naruto has
  about 100 times his chakra (Naruto is an Uzumaki). The Lee/Might line should train taijutsu **much**
  more efficiently and have better willpower. Research every clan deeply and make the differences big.
- **The Inuzuka ninja hound:** you start with a **puppy** that grows stronger and bigger as you do. It looks
  like a dog or wolf, in Minecraft-style boxes, and its growth stages are clearly visible. Try different
  models and sizes first. Special attention to the dog's design, skills and growth, researched to be as
  accurate as possible. (The owner wrote "Akimichi" here, but the dog is the Inuzuka clan's; treated as
  the Inuzuka.)

### Hound change (owner, 2026-11-19)
"Make the dog also have traits and many different styles, and I don't want it to be fixed by the 4
stages of growth, just a progressive system where it keeps growing and becoming stronger."
- **Continuous growth:** a growth level that keeps rising. Size and proportions change smoothly, then level
  off at a sensible maximum. Strength keeps climbing with diminishing returns. Abilities unlock at
  milestone levels, not stages.
- **Hound traits:** 2–3 rolled per hound, with at most one weakness.
- **Many styles:** builds or breeds, coat patterns, colours, ear and tail types, markings.

## Clans, round 3 (owner, 2026-11-25): each clan a game changer, lore accurate
After the creator/UI wave. The owner: "I want those clans to each be some great game change, make it lore
accurate. Uzumaki, Senju and Uchiha, for example, should be overpowered as f***, same with Kaguya, etc. Get into
research: canon statements, databooks, manga pages… get as much info as possible before you start working,
and apply it ALL in Minecraft. I'm giving you creative liberties from now on, so get creative."
- **Research first and deeply:** databook entries and stats, canon statements, manga and anime feats, for every
  clan. Cite sources (chapter, episode, databook) in `docs/CLANS.md`, in our own words.
- **Every clan a game changer:** not just percentages. Each clan should change how you play: signature
  mechanics, clan-only techniques and kekkei genkai, awakenings, unique progression, clan items, a look,
  clan NPCs and places.
- **The great clans are overpowered, as in canon:** Uzumaki (vitality, chakra, sealing chains), Senju
  (Wood Release, life force, healing), Uchiha (the Sharingan's whole path), Kaguya (Shikotsumyaku). The
  drawbacks and canon weaknesses still apply. Balance comes from how hard the power is to unlock and its
  costs, not from shrinking it.
- **Creative liberty:** invent mechanics freely where canon is silent, but stay true to the lore. Keep our
  basics: our own art, no copied assets, server-authoritative, data-driven, tests.
- **Owner, follow-up (2026-11-25):** "The 100× statement is indeed 100×: it's that way because Kurama's
  seal is suppressing Naruto's own chakra." Read statements like that generously, for every clan: the
  meaning behind the words and names (Senju = "a thousand skills", master of all things), behaviours that
