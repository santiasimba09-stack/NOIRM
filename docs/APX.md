# NOIRM APX 0.1: "fully usable" (owner's brief, 2026-10-28)

Goal: a complete, playable progression from a fresh character in underwear to a legendary shinobi.
Everything here is decided by the owner (answers of 2026-10-28); ask before changing it.

## Stats
- **Speed:** movement grows **gently** up to Speed 35 (gentler than v0.13); from 35 to 50 movement grows
  only a little and the rest goes into **perception**; past 50 movement stops growing. Applies to
  players, ninjas and crew ("ninjas are getting way too fast").
- **Speed is also trained in combat** (dodges, fast combos, chasing), not only by running.
- **New stat: Durability (DUR).** A share of incoming damage is negated, up to **60%** at the cap,
  scaling smoothly. Trained by **taking hits** in real fights (drills cap at 30 like other stats).
- **Kill XP:** defeating a ninja gives XP to the stats you used most in that fight (taijutsu if you
  punched, speed if you dodged/ran, ninjutsu-related stats if you cast, durability if you tanked…),
  scaled by the enemy's class.

## Ninja classes and the bingo book
- Every ninja, and the player, has a **class D, C, B, A or S** from their power, shown with their name.
  Higher classes give much more XP; missions and bounties use the class.
- **Bingo book** item: lists dangerous rogues (A/S) in the world with **bounties in ryo**.
- **Legendary ninjas** are listed like the others but get **special effects** (an aura, a title).

## Rank, missions, money
- **Ryo** is the currency; village shops use ryo.
- **Missions board** in villages (hunt rogues, escort, deliver, scout…), paid in ryo + rank points.
- **Player rank** genin → chūnin → jōnin → … via missions and **rank-up exams** (e.g. a chūnin exam
  fight); higher ranks unlock missions, shop items and masters.

## Natures and jutsu
- New natures by **all routes**: a chakra-paper test reveals hidden natures; long nature drills;
  rare nature scrolls (ruins, shops); teachers (masters, crew mates who have the nature).
- Learning jutsu: mastery chains (existing), jutsu scrolls, masters.

## Sage Mode
- **Our own sage path:** a hidden sage master in a remote place teaches it; pigment marks around the
  eyes; a strong boost while nature energy lasts; **petrification** if you take in more than your CTL
  allows. Uses the meditation and nature energy we already have.

## More masters and polish
- New masters (ninjutsu, medical, the sage), a guide for new players, a bug and balance pass.

## Death
- **Vanilla only:** nothing extra is lost.

## Follow-ups from v0.13.0 answers
- Wild ninjas: **fewer** (quieter than one group per 1.5 min).
- Long held models for the yari and nodachi; **more chakra-flow weapons** (e.g. a fire nodachi);
  samurai −12% speed stays; **squads defend you** against rogues attacking you.

## UI redesign (owner, 2026-10-29)
- "Make it smoother, make it minimalist while keeping the color palette we established, so it
  doesn't lose too much of the style." Every NOIRM screen and the HUD: fewer boxes and borders, more
  space, one consistent component set, smooth open/close and hover animations, the same palette
  (ash/iron blacks and greys, bone white, blood-red accent, chakra blue/green for chakra).
